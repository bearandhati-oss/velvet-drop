Velvet Drop

Drop. Fill. Bloom.

A calm falling-block puzzle with no ads, no account, and nothing to sign up for. Play offline anywhere.

No ads. No tracking.

Everything stays on your device.

The bloom

Drop gem pieces and fill rows to clear them. Three matching gems side by side in a cleared row bloom, and every connected gem of that color clears with them. Gems fall, rows complete, chains follow.

Three characters, one Style Shop

Play as Akira, Ava, or Grace. Every round pays coins for outfits, hair colors, accessories, pets, poses, and dances. Save looks, pose her in the Photo Booth, and fill the Sticker Book. Nothing in the shop is sold for money.

Four ways to play

Journey across four worlds, Endless, a Daily puzzle that is the same for everyone, and Calm with no rush.

Private by design

No network, no analytics, no accounts. Two optional extras are handled by Apple behind a grown-up check and only add looks and levels. Read the privacy policy.
