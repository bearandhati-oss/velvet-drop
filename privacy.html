Velvet Drop

Privacy Policy

Short version: Velvet Drop collects nothing. There are no ads, no accounts, and no network connection. Everything you do in the game stays on your device.

Last updated October 19, 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. Velvet Drop does not collect, store, or send personal information of any kind. No names, email addresses, location, device identifiers, contacts, or usage data. We have no servers, no analytics, no advertising networks, and no third-party code in the app.

No network

The game does not connect to the internet. It plays fully offline. The only connections ever made from the app are Apple's own: the App Store for purchases, and the Settings app or this website if you tap a link to them from the game.

What stays on your device

Your progress, stars, coins, gems, looks, stickers, Studio decor, and settings are saved only on your iPhone or iPad. Photo Booth pictures are saved to your Photos library when you tap Save, using an add-only permission: Velvet Drop can add a picture, and never reads, browses, or edits your photos. Deleting the app removes everything it saved on the device; pictures you saved to Photos stay in Photos.

Purchases

The two optional extras are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The purchase screen sits behind a grown-up check, and purchases unlock only looks and levels, never an advantage in play. Restore purchases on a new device from Settings in the game.

Children

Velvet Drop is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children. There is no chat, no sharing, no social features, and no way to contact other players.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. The app itself will still collect nothing.
